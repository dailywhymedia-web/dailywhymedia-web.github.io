8 PC game deals worth it this week, from $1.49 to $4.80

November 24, 2026 · Daily Why Media

Photo: nathan-osman on Pixabay

Most cheap PC games are cheap for a reason. These are the ones we'd buy this week: every pick has a Metacritic score of 75 or more, at least 80% positive Steam reviews, and costs under $5 right now. Prices are from CheapShark, which tracks the big PC stores, and we show the cheapest store for each game.

Free this week: Mark of the Deep on Epic

Mark of the Deep is free to keep on the Epic Games Store until Thursday, November 26 at 8 a.m. Pacific. Claim it once and it stays in your library. Epic lists Firestone – Idle Clicker Online RPG and Bread and Fred as next week's free games, from November 26 to December 3.

1. BioShock: $4.80 (was $19.99)

A first-person shooter set in Rapture, a collapsed underwater city, with a story that still gets talked about nearly twenty years on. Metacritic 96 and 93% positive from about 12,800 Steam reviews. The most expensive pick here, and still a bargain.

$4.80 at GameBillet

2. Battlefield 1: $1.99 (was $39.99)

A shooter set in World War I, with big battlefields and a lot of period weapons and vehicles. Metacritic 88 and 86% positive from about 47,500 Steam reviews. A very low price for a game with that many players behind it.

$1.99 on Steam

3. F.E.A.R.: $1.99 (was $9.99)

A 2005 horror shooter known for its slow-motion gunfights and creepy atmosphere. Metacritic 88 and 93% positive from about 8,400 Steam reviews. It looks dated, but the combat still feels good.

$1.99 on Steam

4. Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time: $1.99 (was $9.99)

The 2003 action-platformer where you can rewind time to undo a mistake. Metacritic 89 and 83% positive from about 2,500 Steam reviews. Good for anyone who likes acrobatic puzzle-style levels.

$1.99 on Steam

5. The Swapper: $2.24 (was $14.99)

A puzzle platformer in a dark sci-fi setting where you create clones of yourself to get past obstacles. Metacritic 87 and 93% positive from about 3,400 Steam reviews. Best for players who like to think more than react.

$2.24 on Steam

6. SteamWorld Dig 2: $2.00 (was $19.99)

A 2D game about digging down through the ground, collecting ore and upgrading your gear. Metacritic 85 and 95% positive from about 3,100 Steam reviews. Easy to play in short sessions.

$2.00 at Green Man Gaming

7. Lacuna: A Sci-Fi Noir Adventure: $1.60 (was $15.99)

A detective story in a sci-fi setting where your choices change how the case ends. Metacritic 86 and 92% positive from about 740 Steam reviews. A good fit if you like story-led games more than action.

$1.60 at Green Man Gaming

8. The Last Campfire: $1.49 (was $14.99)

A calm adventure with light puzzles about a lost traveler trying to find the way home. Metacritic 80 and 97% positive from about 6,000 Steam reviews. The cheapest pick this week.

$1.49 at WinGameStore

How we pick

We start from CheapShark's deal ratings, drop anything under 75 on Metacritic or under 80% positive on Steam, and then choose games we can recommend in a sentence. Store prices change fast, so check the price before you buy. Watch Free Game Drop for the daily free games and deals.
