8 PC game deals worth it this week, all under $3

November 17, 2026 · Daily Why Media

Hundreds of PC games are on sale at any time, and most of the cheap ones are cheap for a reason. These are the ones we'd buy this week: every pick has a Metacritic score of 75 or more, at least 80% positive Steam reviews, and costs under $3 right now. Prices are from CheapShark, which tracks the big PC stores.

Free this week: Knightica on Epic

Knightica is free to keep on the Epic Games Store until Thursday, November 19 at 8 a.m. Pacific. Claim it once and it stays in your library. Epic lists Mark of the Deep as next week's free game, from November 19 to 26.

1. Deus Ex: Human Revolution, Director's Cut: $1.99 (was $19.99)

A cyberpunk action RPG where almost every problem has three answers: sneak past it, talk your way through, or fight. Metacritic 91 and 91% positive from about 13,600 Steam reviews. The Director's Cut includes The Missing Link add-on. At $1.99 this is the pick of the week.

$1.99 at WinGameStore

2. Shadow Tactics: Blades of the Shogun: $2.49 (was $39.99)

Real-time stealth tactics in Edo-period Japan: five specialists, guards with visible sight cones, and levels you can solve many ways. 94% positive from about 9,100 Steam reviews. Great if you like planning a perfect run and reloading when it goes wrong.

$2.49 at WinGameStore

3. The Forgotten City: $2.49 (was $24.99)

A time-loop mystery set in an ancient Roman city, where one person's sin dooms everyone. It started as a Skyrim mod and grew into its own game. 95% positive from about 7,500 Steam reviews. Short, around 6 to 8 hours, and best played without spoilers.

$2.49 at IndieGala

4 and 5. Metro 2033 Redux ($1.99) and Metro Exodus ($2.99)

Both Metro games are 90% off at GOG. 2033 Redux is a tight, tense shooter in the Moscow subway after a nuclear war (Metacritic 90). Exodus takes the story above ground into bigger, open levels (Metacritic 82, 87% positive from about 35,000 Steam reviews). Under $5 for both.

Metro 2033 Redux, $1.99 at GOG · Metro Exodus, $2.99 at GOG

6. Psychonauts: $1.49 (was $9.99)

Double Fine's 2005 platformer about a psychic summer camp, where each level takes place inside someone's mind. 95% positive from about 9,100 Steam reviews. The controls show their age, but the writing and level ideas still hold up.

$1.49 at WinGameStore

7. Rogue Legacy: $1.49 (was $14.99)

A 2D castle-crawler where every death passes the run to an heir with random traits, some useful and some silly. 91% positive from about 10,100 Steam reviews. Easy to play in short sessions.

$1.49 at GOG

8. Fly'N: $1.00 (was $9.99)

A small, colorful 2D puzzle platformer from 2012. Metacritic 80 and 92% positive on Steam, though from fewer reviews (about 290). A cheap gamble on something different.

$1.00 at Green Man Gaming

How we pick

We start from CheapShark's deal ratings, drop anything under 75 on Metacritic or under 80% positive on Steam, and then choose games we can recommend in a sentence. Store prices change fast, so check the price before you buy. Watch Free Game Drop for the daily free games and deals.
