Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Daily Why Media runs a small set of YouTube channels. This policy covers this website and the private software ("the app") that uploads our own videos to our own YouTube channels.

What the app accesses

The app signs in with Google only to the YouTube channels we own. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload our videos, set their titles, descriptions and privacy, and read view and subscriber counts for our own channels. It does not access any other person's Google account or data.

What we store

Sign-in tokens for our own channels are stored on our own server and are never shared or sold. We do not collect, store or sell personal data about viewers. Video statistics we read are totals for our own channels.

How long we keep data, and deletion

View, like and subscriber counts for our own channels are kept for up to 36 months to track how our videos do; video titles and other YouTube data in our records are refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Sign-in tokens are kept only while a channel uses the app and are deleted when a channel stops using it. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which we delete that channel's token. To ask us to delete any data, email us at the address below; we reply within 7 days.

This website

This site has no accounts, forms, cookies or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request data as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Links

Some links on the deals page may be affiliate links: if you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The store's own privacy policy applies on their site.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can review apps connected to a Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
