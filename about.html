About Daily Why Media

Daily Why Media is a small independent publisher in the United States. We run 12 short-video channels on YouTube and Facebook and this website. Each channel covers one subject every day: free games and PC game deals, space news, tech, sports, history, money, and the most-spotted plants and animals in each region.

How we make the videos

Every video starts from a public, named source: the Epic Games Store and CheapShark for game prices, NASA for space news, Google Trends for what people are searching, ESPN box scores, Wikipedia, and iNaturalist observations for the nature channels. Software gathers the facts and drafts a script; every script is then checked line by line against its source, and corrected or thrown out, before it is voiced and published. Voices are computer-generated. Images and music are licensed and credited.

Game deals

Free Game Drop is our main channel for gamers. It covers the weekly free game on Epic and the best-reviewed PC games on sale across stores. On this site we post a weekly pick of deals actually worth buying: well-reviewed games at low prices, with a plain reason for each pick. We don't list games just because they are cheap, and no store pays us to include a game.
